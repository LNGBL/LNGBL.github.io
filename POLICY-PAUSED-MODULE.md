# Paused Module — Internal Product Record

**Status: FROZEN**  
**Effective date:** 2026-11-04

## Decision

One previously planned learning module is paused. It is not available for launch, onboarding, purchase, activation, or new learning sessions. This record is an internal operational control, not a public product announcement.

## Human-centred and non-political standard

LangBlue is an educational platform. Product names, visuals, copy, examples, partnerships, and promotions must be selected for clarity, learner benefit, accessibility, and practical usefulness—not political identity, national allegiance, historical nostalgia, ideological symbolism, or state messaging.

Do not use political emblems, flags as ideological branding, military imagery, party slogans, propaganda aesthetics, or historical/political personalities as product mascots. Do not replace one political message with its opposite. Keep educational material factual, proportionate, age-appropriate, and directly relevant to a learning objective.

## Pragmatic release gate

The paused module may only be reconsidered after a documented review of:
1. A real learner need and a clearly defined target group.
2. A coherent curriculum and demonstrable educational value.
3. Total implementation, hosting, moderation, support, and maintenance costs.
4. Clear price, subscription duration, expiry, refund, and support processes.
5. Tested account access, entitlement checks, and receipt flows.
6. Data minimization, consent, retention, export, and deletion controls.
7. Applicable laws and the terms of hosting, payment, and platform providers.
8. A neutral, accessible design based on learning—not politics or national symbolism.

Decisions must use evidence, learner value, operating cost, risk, and maintainability. Do not publish arbitrary “pragmatism percentages” or imply legal certification.

## Freeze controls

- Keep the module out of the public product directory and new purchase/receipt selectors.
- Exclude it from new bundles and default trial grants.
- Keep its product record inactive.
- Prevent direct entry and new learning-event writes.
- Preserve historical records and existing user data; do not rewrite or delete them as part of this freeze.
- Keep the internal identifier only where needed for referential integrity, historical records, and controlled migration.
- Do not add promotional banners, pop-ups, push notifications, or unrelated landing-page notices about this freeze.

## Authority to unfreeze

Only the platform owner may authorize reactivation. Reactivation requires a new product, privacy, security, commercial, and content review.
