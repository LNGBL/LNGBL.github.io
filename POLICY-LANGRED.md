# LangRed — Educational Neutrality, Product and Compliance Policy

**Policy status:** Product operating policy  
**Scope:** LangRed Russian-language learning module, its landing-page presentation, sales and subscription flows, learning content, user-generated learning records, and connected LangBlue services.

## 1. Sole product purpose

LangRed exists to teach and support the study of the Russian language. Its product purpose is educational: vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, reading, listening, writing, speaking, language-use examples, cultural literacy where relevant to language comprehension, and structured review.

The product must be useful to a learner who simply wants to learn Russian, regardless of that learner's nationality, residence, political views, or reasons for studying the language, subject always to applicable law and platform restrictions.

## 2. Political neutrality — operational rule

LangRed is not a political campaign, advocacy outlet, geopolitical commentary channel, state communication channel, or vehicle for promoting any national or ideological identity.

The following are prohibited as LangRed product messaging, promotional content, sponsored content, onboarding copy, push notifications, and official social posts:
- Advocacy or propaganda promoting or denouncing a government, political party, military, ideology, nationalist project, or geopolitical faction.
- Recruitment, fundraising, campaigning, political persuasion, or calls to support a side in a current political or military conflict.
- State-sponsored messaging presented as independent language education.
- Using Russian-language education to promote Russophilia, or using anti-Russian sentiment as a product identity or marketing tactic.
- Political slogans, symbols, or visual framing that reasonably imply the platform endorses a state, government, military, party, or side in a conflict.

No country flag is required to represent the Russian language. LangRed's loading screen and core brand presentation should use neutral educational or linguistic imagery rather than national or military symbols.

## 3. Necessary educational context is not political promotion

A language course cannot be made pedagogically complete by pretending that political, historical, or social vocabulary does not exist. Such material may appear only when it has a clear language-learning purpose, such as explaining a word, register, grammar construction, reading passage, or historically situated expression.

When such context is necessary:
- Keep the learning objective explicit and primary.
- Use factual, proportionate, age-appropriate, and non-sensational wording.
- Distinguish a language example from endorsement of the statement it contains.
- Do not praise, excuse, justify, recruit for, or propagandize on behalf of a government, armed actor, political movement, or ideology.
- Where a contested historical or political claim is essential to comprehension, identify it as a claim or viewpoint and provide adequate context rather than presenting advocacy as fact.
- Prefer ordinary, everyday, literary, scientific, professional, and travel-related language examples.

The presence of a political term in a dictionary or neutral reading exercise is not, by itself, political advertising. The decisive question is whether the material is serving a genuine educational objective or attempting to persuade the learner politically.

## 4. Fair treatment of learners and legal pragmatism

- Do not exclude, downgrade, profile, or stigmatize a learner merely because they speak Russian, study Russian, have Russian nationality, or are perceived to have a particular political view.
- Do not infer legal residence, sanctions status, or eligibility from language, ethnicity, nationality, or one approximate IP signal.
- Apply sanctions, export-control, payment-provider, privacy, consumer-protection, and platform rules only after identifying the actual service, entity, person, location, transaction, and restriction in scope.
- Where a legal or provider restriction genuinely applies, restrict only the affected service, transaction, feature, or account relationship to the extent required; do not turn it into a broad cultural or linguistic ban.
- If a legal question is uncertain or materially affects access or payment, pause that specific transaction or feature for review and obtain qualified advice. Do not improvise a blanket prohibition.
- This policy is an operational product standard, not a legal opinion or certification.

## 5. Landing page, marketing and sales

- Describe LangRed as a Russian-language learning product. Do not use geopolitical positioning, national rivalry, war-related urgency, or political identity to attract customers.
- Product cards, landing copy, pricing, receipts, subscription labels, and trial messaging must describe educational features and access periods only.
- Pricing and access terms must be transparent: selected product(s), duration, total price, trial start/end, renewal or expiry conditions, and any applicable discount.
- A learner should be able to find LangRed from the main LangBlue product directory and select it in the central purchase-request flow.
- The same central account, subscription verification, activation process, and account-rights workflow apply. Do not create a separate political, nationality-based, or language-based payment path.
- Never imply that a receipt is proof of payment or activation; activation is confirmed by the central backend.

## 6. Privacy and learning data

- Core learning must remain usable without optional behavioral research or marketing analytics.
- Collect only data necessary for account operation, saving learning progress, subscription/access checks, security, and user-requested features.
- Optional research or behavioral analytics require the applicable central consent setting and must not be repurposed for political profiling, nationality inference, or political advertising.
- Keep LangRed learning state namespaced under `langred_ru`; use the central account and owner-scoped data controls.
- Do not expose service-role or secret keys in browser code.
- Preserve access, correction, export, and deletion rights through the central account workflow, as applicable law requires.

## 7. Review and enforcement

Before publishing a LangRed lesson, landing-page change, campaign, or sponsored item, ask:
1. Is there a concrete Russian-language learning objective?
2. Is the material factual and proportionate?
3. Could a reasonable learner interpret it as political advocacy or state endorsement?
4. Is the same educational standard applied without anti-Russian or pro-Russian bias?
5. Is the content lawful and consistent with platform and payment-provider rules?

If the content fails the educational-purpose test or functions as political persuasion, do not publish it in LangRed. Escalate genuinely uncertain legal cases for review instead of applying broad, speculative restrictions.

## 8. Central integration

LangRed uses the LangBlue central account, authentication, product catalogue, subscription and trial checks, activation, purchase-request flow, and shared learning infrastructure. Product identifier: `langred`. Learning-state namespace: `langred_ru`.

The policy must be reviewed when relevant laws, sanctions rules, platform terms, payment-provider rules, or the product's data practices materially change.
