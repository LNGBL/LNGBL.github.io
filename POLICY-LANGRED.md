# LangRed — Frozen Product Record

**Status: FROZEN — not available for launch, onboarding, purchase, activation, or new learning sessions.**  
**Effective date:** 2026-11-04

## 1. Decision

LangRed is paused. This is an operational freeze, not a deletion of the codebase, user records, or historical subscription records. Do not develop, market, sell, activate, or expose this product until an explicit release decision is recorded by the platform owner.

## 2. Practical positioning standard

If the product is reconsidered, it must be evaluated only as a practical Russian-language learning service. Its value proposition must be measurable and learner-facing: useful curriculum, accurate language content, clear progression, reliable review, transparent pricing, support cost, and legal/provider feasibility.

The product must not adopt Soviet, Marxist, communist, nationalist, state-promotional, military, or geopolitical visual language or messaging. Do not use flags, state emblems, political slogans, ideological portraits, military imagery, propaganda aesthetics, or claims of cultural/political allegiance. Do not replace these with anti-Russian or anti-Soviet campaigning; the product is educational, not political.

## 3. Pragmatic release gate

No relaunch until all of the following are documented:
1. A concrete learner problem and a defined target segment.
2. A differentiated curriculum and evidence that it is useful.
3. A realistic implementation and maintenance cost.
4. Clear pricing, subscription duration, refund/expiry handling, and support ownership.
5. A functioning central-account and access-control test.
6. Privacy-minimizing data collection and tested deletion/export paths.
7. A review of applicable legal, hosting, platform, and payment-provider constraints.
8. A neutral visual identity based on typography, learning progress, and language examples—not national or ideological symbolism.

A relaunch decision must be based on user value, operating feasibility, risk, and cost. Do not use an arbitrary “pragmatism percentage” as a legal, scientific, or compliance claim.

## 4. Freeze controls

- Remove the product from the public product directory and all new purchase/receipt selectors.
- Exclude it from new bundles and default trial grants.
- Mark the central product record inactive.
- Remove direct-entry shortcuts and prevent the old page from starting a learning session.
- Preserve existing user learning data and historical subscription records. Do not revoke or rewrite existing entitlements solely because of this freeze.
- Do not delete the product identifier or namespace; they are retained for data integrity and any future owner-approved migration.
- Do not collect new LangRed-specific learning events while frozen.

## 5. Reconsideration

Only the platform owner may authorize unfreezing. Before that, keep the product unavailable and keep its code/data isolated from active LangBlue products. Any future launch requires a fresh product, design, security, privacy, and commercial review.
